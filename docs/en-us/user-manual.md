## 1. 📲 App Installation (PWA)

CofreAdmin (Self-Custodial Web3 Personal Finance) is built as a state-of-the-art **Progressive Web App (PWA)**.

### On iPhone / iPad (Safari):
1. Open the CofreAdmin link in the **Safari** browser.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square icon with an upward arrow in the toolbar).
3. Scroll down and select **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top right corner.
5. Done! The CofreAdmin icon will appear on your home screen like a native app, with no URL bar.

### On Android (Chrome):
1. Open the CofreAdmin link in **Google Chrome**.
2. Tap the bottom banner **"Install App"** or click the **3-dot menu** in the top right corner.
3. Choose **"Install app"** (or *"Add to Home screen"*).
4. Confirm the installation. The app will launch in full-screen mode with its own custom icon and quick shortcuts.

## 3. 💵 Your Web3 Digital Wallet & FX Rate

Upon first launch, CofreAdmin generates a **100% self-custodial** crypto wallet compatible with the **Base network (Ethereum Layer 2 built by Coinbase)**.

* **Primary Currency (USDC):** Your wallet balance is held in **USD Coin (USDC)**, a stablecoin issued and pegged 1:1 to the US Dollar.
* **Real-Time Exchange Rates:** The main balance card displays your funds in **USDC ($)** alongside local fiat equivalents (e.g., **BRL / R$**) based on live market spot rates updated automatically.
* **Free Balance vs. Locked Vault Balance:**
  * **Total Balance:** The total amount of USDC sitting in your wallet.
  * **Vault Balance:** The reserved funds locked inside your goal vaults/savings jars.
  * **Available Free Balance:** Liquid funds ready for immediate spending and transfers. You will never accidentally spend money set aside for your goals!
* **Network Switching (Mainnet / Sepolia Testnet):** For testing or learning purposes, you can toggle between *Base Mainnet* and *Base Sepolia* testnet directly in settings.

## 4. 📥 How to Receive Funds (USDC on Base)

Receive peer-to-peer transfers, freelance payouts, or deposit funds from any major centralized exchange (Coinbase, Binance, Bybit, Mercado Bitcoin):

1. On the home screen or Wallet tab, tap the **"Receive"** button 📲.
2. A sleek modal will pop up displaying your **Official QR Code** and your **Base Wallet Address** (`0x...` format).
3. **To receive from someone else:** Ask them to scan your QR code using their wallet app.
4. **To deposit from an exchange:**
   * Copy your address by clicking **"Copy Address"**.
   * In your exchange withdrawal dashboard, request a withdrawal of **USDC** and strictly select the **Base Network**.
5. **Instant Finality:** In under 2 to 3 seconds, the transaction settles on-chain on Base, and your app balance updates in real time!

## 5. 📤 How to Send Payments & Transfers

Send digital dollars anywhere in the world within seconds, friction-free, paying less than **$0.02 USD** in network gas fees:

1. On the home screen, tap **"Send"** 📤.
2. **Specify the Recipient:**
   * Paste the destination address (`0x...`). The app automatically validates if the address is compatible with the Base network.
   * Or tap the **Camera / QR Code** icon to scan the recipient's wallet code instantly.
3. **Specify the Amount:**
   * Type the value in **USDC ($)** or toggle with 1 tap to input local fiat values with automatic conversion.
4. **No Native ETH Required for Gas:**
   * Thanks to account abstraction technology (EIP-3009 and Circle Paymaster), **you do not need to hold ETH in your wallet** to cover transaction gas fees! Micro-fees are deducted directly in fractional USDC.
5. **Review & Broadcast:**
   * Review amounts, fees, and recipient details on the confirmation screen.
   * Confirm the transaction using your device's biometric authentication or app PIN.
6. **Digital Receipt:**
   * Upon successful transfer, you receive a detailed official receipt containing the transaction hash (`TxHash`), timestamp, value, and a direct link to view the execution on the public block explorer **BaseScan**.

## 6. 🎯 Smart Vaults (Savings & Goals)

**Goal Vaults** help you segregate daily spending money from your long-term milestones and future plans:

1. Access the **"Vaults"** tab on the bottom navigation menu.
2. Tap **"+ New Vault"** 🚀.
3. **Customize Your Goal:**
   * Pick a goal emoji (e.g., ✈️ *Travel*, 🎮 *Gaming Setup*, 🎸 *Concert*, 🚗 *Car*, 🛡️ *Emergency Fund*).
   * Name your vault and set a target amount (in USDC or fiat).
4. **Deposit Funds:**
   * Tap on your newly created vault and select **"Deposit"**. Type the amount you wish to transfer from your free balance into the vault.
   * The visual progress bar updates instantly to show completion percentage.
5. **Withdraw Anytime:**
   * Needed your funds back? Tap the vault and select **"Withdraw"**. The funds return immediately to your available free balance with zero penalties or lock-up periods.
6. **Goal Celebration:**
   * Upon hitting 100% of your target, the app celebrates with confetti animations and a special achievement push notification!
7. **Edit or Delete:**
   * You can rename, change emojis, or delete a vault you no longer need (any remaining balance is safely returned to your main balance).

## 10. 🛡️ Security, Biometrics & Private Key Backup

CofreAdmin is engineered with institutional-grade cryptographic security standards:

### 1. Device Lock / Biometrics (WebAuthn)
* In the **Settings** screen, you can enable **App Biometric Lock**.
* Upon reopening the app or executing high-value transactions, it will prompt for **Face ID**, **Touch ID**, or your device **PIN**.

### 2. True Self-Custody & Private Key Backup
* Your private key is never transmitted to backend servers nor exposed to third parties. It is securely encrypted and stored within your browser's local hardware storage.
* **How to Backup:**
  1. Go to **Settings** > **Wallet & Security**.
  2. Tap **"Backup Private Key"**.
  3. Authenticate with your biometrics/password.
  4. Copy your cryptographic key (`0x...`) and store it securely (in a password manager or physical notebook).
* **Importing into External Wallets:**
  * With this key, you retain the freedom to import your wallet into any Web3-native app (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, Phantom), guaranteeing your funds are 100% yours and never locked into a single platform.

## 12. 🔒 Privacy, Global Compliance & Account Deletion

CofreAdmin fully respects international data protection regulations (such as LGPD, GDPR, and CCPA) and user data sovereignty:

### Permanent Account & Data Deletion:
You retain the irrevocable right to wipe your personal data at any time.
1. Navigate to **Settings** and scroll to the bottom of the page.
2. Under **Privacy & Data**, tap **"Delete My Account and Data"**.
3. **What happens upon confirmation:**
   * **Cloud Purge:** All operational records, categories, goals history, and metadata stored in the database (`userData`) are permanently and definitively destroyed.
   * **Device Cleanup:** Local storage (`cache`, `localStorage`, and session data) is completely wiped clean.
   * **Compliance Audit Trail:** In strict compliance with regulatory standards (such as Art. 16 of Brazilian LGPD, GDPR compliance exceptions, and equivalent global frameworks), the system generates and archives an immutable, anonymized legal audit log (`legalAuditArchive`) proving to regulatory bodies that your data was lawfully discarded.
   * **Wallet Notice:** Remember to transfer your USDC balances to an external wallet prior to deletion, or ensure you have safely backed up your Private Key if you intend to access those funds later.

## 13. ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the Base network?
Base is an Ethereum Layer 2 (L2) blockchain incubated by **Coinbase** and secured by Ethereum. It settles transactions almost instantaneously (around 2 seconds) for fractions of a cent in fees.

### Do I need to buy Ethereum (ETH) for gas fees?
**No!** CofreAdmin implements EIP-3009 gas abstraction and sponsorship. When you transfer USDC, the minuscule network fee is deducted directly from the USDC transfer itself.

### Are my funds locked or controlled by CofreAdmin?
**No.** The app is strictly non-custodial (self-custodial). Only you hold the cryptographic keys controlling your funds. Neither the development team nor backend servers have the technical capability to freeze, block, or access your balances.

### Is CofreAdmin open-source software?
**Yes!** CofreAdmin is 100% free and open-source software distributed under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0-or-later)**. This ensures complete transparency: anyone can publicly review the codebase to verify there are no hidden fees, backdoors, or custodial risks over your assets.

### What happens if I lose my phone?
If you use a **Google-linked** account, you can log back in instantly on a new device. To ensure unrestricted access to your on-chain assets under any circumstance, always keep your **Private Key Backup** safely stored in a secure location.
